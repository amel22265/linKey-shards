Social Engineering & Human Targeting

You've probably heard the advice not to trust or be pressured by anything you receive unexpectedly — attackers know this, and they target the human side of security first.

Phishing: Fake emails or messages designed to trick people into giving up passwords or money. These messages often use manipulative language to pressure people into revealing sensitive information, and may contain malware disguised as a normal file or familiar link.

Why it matters Phishing remains the single most common way attackers first get in.

Spear Phishing / Whaling: Highly targeted phishing aimed at specific staff members or high-profile executives.

Software & Code Exploits

Malware: Harmful software — viruses, worms, spyware — built to damage systems.

Ransomware: Malware that locks or encrypts files until a ransom is paid.

SQL Injection: Inserting malicious code into website input fields to steal or alter database information.

Network Disruption

DDoS (Distributed Denial-of-Service): Flooding a server with fake traffic to crash it or take it offline.

Access & Identity Attacks

Man-in-the-Middle (MitM): Secretly intercepting or altering messages between two parties who believe their communication is private.

Credential Stuffing: Using stolen username/password lists from past data breaches to log into unrelated accounts.

Key Vocabulary
