What you'll find here

LinKey Shards is a cybersecurity learning platform that helps you learn, forge, and test your technical and soft skills, so you can start or grow in cybersecurity. With us, you'll get familiar with concepts, tools, working techniques, and mindset, test yourself with quizzes, get a CV built by us to fit you and the job you want to apply to, and prepare for your next role effectively and easily.

Our platform's approach to learning rests on one belief: gaining a skill, a job, access, or any goal — no matter how big or small — happens step by step. Every step, every small one you complete, gets you closer to your dream.

"Shard by shard, until the badge is whole."

That's why we reward you with shards whenever you finish a course, a quiz, or any action — like logging in or installing a tool on your own. These shards build into a badge, marking a milestone, a bigger goal reached, until you've collected enough to reach your final goal: landing a job, growing in your current one, and using your cybersecurity knowledge to build a successful career.
